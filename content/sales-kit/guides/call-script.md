title: The 15-minute call
---

A short call to find out whether you can help, show the demo, and agree a next step. Keep it to 15 minutes; book a second call if they want more.

## Before the call

- Run the website check on their current site and have the report open.
- Open the demo for their group: {{demo}}.
- Read their Google listing and social pages: what they sell, their reviews, their busiest channel.

## 1. Open (1 minute)

"Thanks for making time, {{prospect}}. I've got 15 minutes blocked. I'd like to ask a few questions about {{business}}, show you a quick example, and if it looks useful we can agree what happens next. Sound good?"

## 2. Ask (5 minutes)

Ask, then listen. Write down their words; you'll use them in the follow-up.

1. "How do customers find you today?"
2. "What does your website do for you right now? Bookings, sales, enquiries?"
3. "What tools do you pay for to run the business online?"
4. "Who updates the site, and how long does it take?"
5. "If you could fix one thing about it, what would it be?"

## 3. Show (5 minutes)

Share your screen with the demo. Show only the three things from their group's playbook that match what they said in step 2. Use their words: "You said bookings come in by phone; here's what it looks like when they book online."

## 4. Price (2 minutes)

"For {{business}}, I'd suggest the {{plan}} plan. It's {{price}} a month, and that includes hosting, updates, backups and support from me." Then stop and let them answer.

## 5. Agree a next step (2 minutes)

Pick one:
- "Shall I set up your site with your logo and details so you can see it for real?"
- "I'll send a summary by email today. Can we talk again on {{day}}?"
- If they're ready: "I'll send you the sign-up link now: {{shop}}."

## After the call

Send the follow-up email within the hour, using their words from step 2 and the next step you agreed. Add them to your CRM with their group and stage.
