This guide is for you, not for your customers. The numbers are suggestions; set your own prices on the Sales Kit page and every playbook and message uses them.

## Your cost per site

You pay a fixed monthly cost per site (Small size, monthly billing):

| Plan | Your cost | Volume 3-9 sites (10% off) | 10-24 sites (20% off) | 25+ sites (30% off) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Website | $12 | $10.80 | $9.60 | $8.40 |
| Bookings | $19 | $17.10 | $15.20 | $13.30 |
| Store | $29 | $26.10 | $23.20 | $20.30 |
| Community & courses | $39 | $35.10 | $31.20 | $27.30 |
| Everything | $79 | $71.10 | $63.20 | $55.30 |

Medium sites cost 2x and Large 4x. Yearly billing costs 10x the monthly price, so customers get two months free. Resellers pay 40% less than these prices.

## Suggested prices

Most businesses compare you with what they pay today: a designer's monthly care plan, a hosted builder, or a mix of tools for bookings, email and courses. Price against that, not against your cost.

| Plan | Suggested price | Your profit at list cost | With a setup fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website | $49 a month | $37 a month | $199 to $499 one-time |
| Bookings | $79 a month | $60 a month | $299 to $699 |
| Store | $99 a month | $70 a month | $499 to $999 |
| Community & courses | $129 a month | $90 a month | $499 to $999 |
| Everything | $249 a month | $170 a month | $999 to $2,499 |

A setup fee pays for your time to add their content, logo and products. Skip it when the customer is happy to fill the site in themselves, or use it as the discount you offer to close.

## Ways to package it

- **Care plan:** one monthly price that includes the site, updates, backups and a set number of small changes each month.
- **Done for you:** a setup fee plus the monthly price, for customers who want to hand everything over.
- **Do it yourself:** the monthly price only. The customer picks a design and fills it in, and you answer questions.
- **Yearly:** offer two months free for paying a year up front. You pay 10x monthly too, so your margin stays the same and you get paid sooner.

## A worked example

Ten customers on Website at $49 a month bring in $490 a month. At 3-9 sites your cost is $10.80 each; at 10 sites it drops to $9.60, so ten sites cost you $96 and leave $394 a month before your own time.

## Rules for regulated products

For shops selling regulated products (supplements, CBD, vape, firearms accessories and similar), the customer brings their own payment account, and their site must not make health or legal claims. Price these higher for the extra care, and never take on peptides.
