## Suggested prices

Most businesses compare you with what they pay today: a designer's monthly care plan, a hosted builder, or a mix of tools for bookings, email and courses. Price against that, not against your cost.

{{prices.suggested}}

A setup fee pays for your time to add their content, logo and products. Skip it when the customer is happy to fill the site in themselves, or use it as the discount you offer to close.

## Ways to package it

- **Care plan:** one monthly price that includes the site, updates, backups and a set number of small changes each month.
- **Done for you:** a setup fee plus the monthly price, for customers who want to hand everything over.
- **Do it yourself:** the monthly price only. The customer picks a design and fills it in, and you answer questions.
- **Yearly:** offer {{prices.free}} for paying a year up front. You pay {{prices.yearly}} monthly too, so your margin stays the same and you get paid sooner.

## Rules for regulated products

For shops selling regulated products (supplements, CBD, vape, firearms accessories and similar), the customer brings their own payment account, and their site must not make health or legal claims. Price these higher for the extra care, and never take on peptides.
