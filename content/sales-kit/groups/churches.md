## Who

Churches, ministries and other faith communities. Most have a volunteer or a busy staff member running the website, giving through one tool, events through another, and a weekly email through a third.

The best prospects are growing, run several groups or ministries, and want online giving that is simple for members.

## Where to find them

- Churches in your area on Google Maps, especially ones with an outdated site or only a Facebook page
- Denomination and network directories
- Pastors' networks and ministerial associations
- Church tech meetups, hackathons and volunteer groups
- Christian school and ministry directories

## What to lead with

**Their pain:** a website nobody has time to update, giving and events in separate tools, a weekly email that takes hours.

**Your answer:** "Events, online giving, small groups and your weekly email on one simple site, and nobody at the church has to run the tech."

Three things to show:
1. Online giving, one-time and recurring, into the church's own account.
2. Events and small groups with sign-up.
3. Sermons and a weekly email from the same place.

## Objections

- **"Our volunteer built our site."** Your volunteer gets their evenings back. I look after updates and security; your team posts events and sermons.
- **"We already use a giving app."** Giving can move into the site, so members give where they already find events and sermons.
- **"We have a small budget."** One monthly price replaces several tools. I'll show the numbers side by side.
