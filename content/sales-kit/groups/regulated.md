## Who

Businesses that sell legal products many platforms turn away or watch closely: supplements and nutrition, CBD and hemp, vape and smoke shops, licensed alcohol, firearms accessories. Many have had a store shut down or a payment account frozen.

The best prospects already have a merchant account that allows their products, or know a processor who will give them one.

**Before you sell:** never promise anything about health, legality in a given place, or approval by a processor. The store owner is responsible for what they sell and where. Peptides are not accepted.

## Where to find them

- Local supplement, vape, CBD and smoke shops on Google Maps, especially ones with no website or a broken one
- Trade shows and their exhibitor lists
- Industry associations and their member directories
- High-risk payment processors and consultants, who can refer merchants to you
- Facebook and Reddit groups for merchants in these industries

## What to lead with

**Their pain:** platforms that close stores without warning, payment accounts frozen, nowhere to move quickly.

**Your answer:** "A store on your own domain where you bring your own payment account. Your site doesn't depend on a big platform's product rules."

Three things to show:
1. Checkout with the payment account they already have, including card processors that accept their industry.
2. Age check and terms pages that can be added to the store.
3. Their customer list stays theirs, with follow-up emails ready.

## Objections

- **"I don't have a processor."** Talk to a processor that works with your industry first. The store connects to it once you are approved.
- **"Will you guarantee my store stays up?"** It runs on your own domain and your own payment account, not a marketplace's product rules. You are still responsible for selling legally where you ship.
- **"Can you write my product claims?"** No health or legal claims come from me. Your product pages say what you are allowed to say.
