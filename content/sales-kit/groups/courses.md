## Who

People who teach and want to sell it: coaches, consultants, tutors, fitness and yoga teachers, music and art teachers, YouTubers and podcasters with an audience, and anyone running a paid group on Facebook, Discord or Skool.

The best prospects already have an audience and pay for two or more tools today (a course platform, an email tool, a community app, a payment link).

## Where to find them

- YouTube, podcast and Instagram creators in a niche you know, with links to a course or "join my program"
- Skool, Discord and Facebook paid groups
- Local studios and schools on Google Maps (yoga, dance, music, tutoring, driving)
- Podcast guest lists and event speaker lists
- Creators selling on a big course platform under the platform's name

## What to lead with

**Their pain:** paying several monthly tools, a platform's cut or branding on their course, students and members scattered across apps.

**Your answer:** "Your courses, community and email list on one site with your name on it, for one monthly price."

Three things to show:
1. Courses with lessons, quizzes and progress tracking.
2. A members' community on their own site instead of a separate app.
3. Email to students and leads from the same place.

## Objections

- **"My audience is used to the app I'm on."** Members log in once on your own site and get everything in one place. I move your content and invite them.
- **"I'm not technical."** I set it up and look after it. You record lessons and post in your community.
- **"What about payments?"** One-time, payment plans and subscriptions all go to your own payment account.
