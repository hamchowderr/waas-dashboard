## Who

Software companies and tools whose own customers need a website: booking and point-of-sale systems, CRMs, membership and scheduling tools, IT providers, and app builders. They want to offer a site as part of their product without running WordPress hosting themselves.

The best prospects have customers asking "can you do my website too?" and a team that can call an API or set up a Zapier, Make or n8n flow.

## Where to find them

- Software for local businesses (salons, gyms, restaurants, trades): their partner and integration pages
- IT service providers and managed service providers on Google Maps
- Product directories and integration marketplaces
- Developer and no-code communities
- LinkedIn: product managers and partnership leads at small software companies

## What to lead with

**Their pain:** customers want a website and they have to say no, or send them somewhere else and lose part of the relationship.

**Your answer:** "Offer every customer a website from inside your own product. A new customer signs up, and their site is made automatically, under your brand."

Three things to show:
1. A site made automatically when a customer signs up (API, Zapier, Make, n8n or HighLevel).
2. The site suspended or removed automatically when a customer leaves.
3. One monthly bill for all sites.

## Objections

- **"We'd build it ourselves."** You'd also host, update, secure and back up every site. This gives you the sites without the operations work.
- **"Our customers aren't on WordPress."** They don't need to know it is. They get a site they can edit in a visual editor.
- **"What about scale?"** Sites are made and switched off by automation, and pricing drops at volume.
