## Who

Marketers, designers and consultants whose clients need websites, but who don't want to host, update or fix them. That includes marketing and ad firms, social media managers, SEO consultants, designers, business coaches, and HighLevel agencies who sell a sub-account to every client.

The best prospects already have clients paying them monthly, and turn down website work or hand it to someone else.

## Where to find them

- Marketing, social media and SEO businesses on Google Maps and LinkedIn
- HighLevel Facebook groups and communities
- Freelance platforms: designers and marketers offering "website" as an add-on
- Local business networking groups (BNI, chamber mixers)
- Communities and podcasts for marketers and designers

## What to lead with

**Their pain:** clients ask for a website and they either say no, build one they then have to maintain for free, or send the client somewhere else.

**Your answer:** "Offer websites to your clients under your own name. I host, update and back them up; you keep the client and add a monthly line to every invoice."

Three things to show:
1. A client's site goes live in minutes from a ready-made design.
2. Updates, security, backups and support handled for them.
3. Their own price on top, so every site adds monthly income.

**For HighLevel agencies:** every sub-account they sell can come with a real WordPress site, made by their HighLevel workflow and connected to that sub-account as a CRM.

## Objections

- **"I already build sites."** Keep building the ones you enjoy. Use this for clients who need a good site fast and want it looked after monthly.
- **"My clients will find out who's behind it."** They see your name on the login, the emails and the dashboard.
- **"What if a client leaves?"** The site is theirs to keep or move; you're never locked in a contract with me per client.
