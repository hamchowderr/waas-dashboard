## Who

Brands with several locations: franchises, regional chains, and service businesses opening new branches. Each location needs its own page, bookings and local search presence, while the brand wants the same look everywhere.

The best prospects have 5 to 50 locations and a marketing lead who is tired of each location doing its own thing.

## Where to find them

- Franchise directories and franchise expos
- Regional chains on Google Maps (search the brand name and count the locations)
- LinkedIn: franchise marketing managers, franchise development and operations leads
- Franchise consultants and brokers, as referral partners
- Local franchise owners, who often want a better site than the brand gives them

## What to lead with

**Their pain:** every location with a different site or none, bookings in different tools, the brand losing control of how it looks.

**Your answer:** "The same site for every location, each with its own bookings and domain, managed from one place."

Three things to show:
1. A new location's site made in minutes from the brand's template.
2. Bookings per location, with its own staff and hours.
3. One monthly bill for all locations.

## Objections

- **"Our franchise agreement covers the website."** Ask what locations get today. Many brands give one page per location and no bookings; this can sit alongside it or replace it.
- **"Each owner wants something different."** The brand look stays fixed; each location changes its own hours, staff, offers and photos.
- **"We have a lot of locations."** Volume pricing applies, and new locations are made from the template, not built by hand.
