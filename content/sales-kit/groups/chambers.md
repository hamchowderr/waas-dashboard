## Who

Groups with members: chambers of commerce, trade and professional associations, business networks, clubs and nonprofits. They run events, collect dues and keep a member directory, often across several tools and a dated website.

The best prospects have a few hundred members, a small staff, and a board that wants to offer members more.

## Where to find them

- Your state's chamber directory and the chambers in nearby towns
- Trade and professional associations in industries you know
- Local events, mixers and award nights: the organisers are your prospects
- Nonprofit directories
- Chamber and association conferences

## What to lead with

**Their pain:** a member directory, event sign-ups and dues spread over several tools; an old website nobody wants to touch; members asking "what do I get?"

**Your answer:** "Members, events, dues and a member directory on one site your staff can run, plus a website perk you can offer every member business."

Three things to show:
1. A member directory with profiles each member can update.
2. Events with sign-up and payment.
3. Dues and renewals paid online, with reminder emails.

**The extra:** a chamber can offer its member businesses their own website as a member benefit. That is a second sale for you, and a reason for members to renew.

## Objections

- **"We just redid our website."** Keep the look. The member tools can be added alongside it, or moved in when you are ready.
- **"Our board decides."** I'll give you a one-page summary and a demo link to share with them.
- **"Our members aren't technical."** Members only sign in to renew, sign up for events and update their profile.
