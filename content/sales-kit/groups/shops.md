## Who

People who already sell something and want their own shop: Etsy, Amazon and eBay sellers, market and craft fair stalls, boutiques with a shop window and no online store, and makers selling through Instagram DMs.

The best prospects already have sales and a following. They feel the marketplace fees and the rules, and they have no list of their own customers.

## Where to find them

- Etsy shops in your area or niche (search a product, filter by shop location)
- Craft fairs, farmers' markets and pop-up markets: the stall holders, and the organiser's vendor list
- Instagram and TikTok sellers who take orders by DM ("DM to order", "link in bio")
- Facebook groups for Etsy, Shopify and handmade sellers
- Local boutiques on Google Maps with no website or only a Facebook page

## What to lead with

**Their pain:** marketplace fees on every sale, rules that change, no way to email past buyers, a shop that looks like everyone else's.

**Your answer:** "Your own shop on your own domain, where you keep the customer and the sale. I set it up and look after it; you just sell."

Three things to show:
1. Checkout with their own payment account, so the money goes straight to them.
2. Their buyers land in their own email list, with follow-up emails already written.
3. Products, shipping and discounts they can change themselves.

### Social and direct messages

**Instagram or Facebook DM:** Love your work! Do you sell anywhere besides DMs? I build simple shops for makers so people can buy without messaging you first. Happy to show you an example.

**Post for your page:** Selling on a marketplace? Every sale pays a fee, and every buyer belongs to the marketplace. Your own shop keeps both. I set them up for makers and small brands; message me for a free demo.

## Objections

- **"Etsy brings me customers."** Keep Etsy. Your own shop is where repeat buyers and your social followers go, without the fee.
- **"I can't run a website."** You won't. I set it up and look after updates, security and backups. You add products and ship orders.
- **"Shopify is cheaper."** Compare with the apps you would add for email, reviews and subscriptions. Here they are included at {{price.store}} a month.
